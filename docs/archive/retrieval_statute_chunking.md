ran no embedding model and used no Groq. Companion to
[`retrieval_redesign.md`](../retrieval_redesign.md), section 1.4.

894 PDF-derived statutes were surveyed: **770** split into sections,
**75** split into sections but flagged for review (coverage below 90%), and
**49** falling back to fixed-size chunks.

The earlier figure of 260 counted statutes with **fewer than 10** headings. Most of those
are short Acts with a few complete sections, and they are section-chunked
(list C). Only the 49 in list A have no usable headings.

The 7 CSV section tables (PPC, CrPC, QSO, Transfer of Property Act, Limitation Act,
MFLO, Police Order) are section-structured by construction and not listed here.

Coverage = body headings found ÷ entries in the statute's own contents list.
"n/a" means the statute has no contents list.
